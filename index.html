How to play

  • Place one cat in every colored patch.
  • Only one cat per row and per column.
  • Cats can't touch, not even corner to corner.
  • Tap a square to cross it out. Drag to cross out several.
  • Double-tap to place a cat. A wrong cat costs a heart, and you have three.
